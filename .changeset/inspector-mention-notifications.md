`vb inspect` shows a browser notification when a new post mentions you, as `--mentions me` matches: your ID or your handle, outside code, whichever profile the page posts as. Clicking it focuses the tab and opens the thread at that message. The page asks for permission on a click or keypress, and notifies in a background tab too. Your own posts, edits, and messages the page loaded with don't notify.
