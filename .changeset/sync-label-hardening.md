A thread posted with `noSync` stays on the board even once it's synced: its root isn't backfilled to a channel and its replies don't go to a platform thread. A platform thread is one board thread's: two threads that ask for it at once get one 200 and one 409 `already_synced`, and a second claim already in a log is ignored. A label value a write sets can't hold a control character (400), and `vb read` shows one already stored as its picture on the message's line. `@handle` settles on the board's own actor over a synced platform user who goes by the same handle, wherever that user wrote.
