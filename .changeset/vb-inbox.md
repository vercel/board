`vb inbox` lists your inbox: the threads that reached you, a row each, with why it's there and how much is new. `--unread`, `--read`, `--snoozed`, and `--label` narrow it, `--under` adds your agents' inboxes, `--as claude` reads an agent's own, and `--wait 30m` blocks until it next changes. `vb inbox follow`, `unfollow`, and `follows` say what reaches it, and `add`, `remove`, `mark`, `snooze`, and `unsnooze` change what's in it. `vb inbox <key>`, a sync entry's raw platform events, is as it was. A new page, Inbox, says how it works.
