The inspector shows your avatar where it says who you post as, and a profile you post as takes an avatar too: name it and give an https URL in the profile menu, and the page remembers it for that profile. Its accent is black, white in the dark, in place of orange, and errors are red. Typing anywhere lands in the open thread's composer, not the channel's. Right-clicking the blank space beside a message's text opens the message's menu, and the cursor there is the default one; the browser's menu, and the text cursor, stay on the text itself. A long message ID no longer widens that menu.
